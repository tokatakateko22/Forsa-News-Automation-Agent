1 Executive Summary & Core Purpose

The Forsa Financial News Monitoring Agent is an autonomous, production-grade artificial intelligence system engineered specifically for the Chief Executive Officer and Executive Committee of Forsa (Drive Finance), one of Egypt's premier consumer finance and Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) institutions.

The agent actively runs on a daily automation schedule, monitoring Egyptian consumer lending news, competitor moves, and Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA) announcements as they unfold every morning. In addition, via GitHub Actions, the system can be configured or dispatched to generate a weekly consolidated digest, aggregating and synthesizing the entire 7-day picture for Sunday executive committee meetings.

⚠️ The Information Bottleneck
  • Information Overload: Hundreds of irrelevant daily articles on central bank interest rates, commercial bank deposits, and commodity prices.
  • Syndicated Repetition: A single corporate press release syndicated across 10+ Egyptian portals with minor headline variations.
  • Transliteration Inconsistencies: Brand names distorted in translation (e.g., Souhoola as SooLa, valU as Valiu).
  • AI Hallucination Risk: Standard chat LLMs inventing speculative opinions rather than grounded regulatory facts.
✅ The Agent's Solution
  • Dual Cadence Flexibility: Runs daily for active morning intelligence; configurable weekly via GitHub Actions.
  • 5-Tier Deduplication: Groups syndicated media coverage into unified canonical events.
  • Deterministic Brand Dictionary: Normalizes Egyptian fintech names to canonical corporate spellings.
  • Strict Factuality: Grounded summaries containing strictly numbers, dates, decisions, and source links.
💡 Architectural Principle: Why This System is NOT a Chatbot
The Forsa News Agent is completely headless, non-conversational, and autonomous. It does not wait for user prompts. Built on LangGraph, it is an autonomous state machine that triggers automatically on its scheduled run (daily morning flow or weekly GitHub Action dispatch), executes an end-to-end 9-stage data and AI pipeline, updates a persistent PostgreSQL database ledger to guarantee 100% idempotency, delivers a curated intelligence digest to the executive inbox, and cleanly terminates.

2 Domain Scope & Dual-Cadence Scheduling

A primary differentiator of the Forsa News Agent is its strict domain filtering. Standard financial aggregators fail because they indiscriminately forward macroeconomic commentary, commercial bank deposit updates, and commodity price trends. The Forsa agent is calibrated strictly for an Egyptian consumer lending and BNPL institution.

⚖️ Strategic Distinction: Why Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) News is Excluded
Forsa is a Non-Banking Financial Institution (NBFI) licensed and regulated by the Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA) pursuant to Egyptian Consumer Finance Law No. 18 of 2020. Forsa is not a commercial bank regulated by the Central Bank of Egypt (CBE). Consequently, CBE Monetary Policy Committee rate decisions, commercial banking reserve requirements, and interbank liquidity operations are intentionally filtered out. This keeps executive briefings strictly focused on consumer lending, BNPL installment regulations, credit caps, and direct market competitors.

Monitoring Scope Boundary Matrix

In-Scope Intelligence (Retained & Prioritized) Out-of-Scope Noise (Filtered & Dropped)
REGULATORY Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA): Circulars, supervisory decrees, board decisions, and licensing guidelines. DROPPED Central Bank of Egypt (CBE): Commercial banking interest rates, MPC policy statements, and reserve ratios.
LENDING Consumer Finance Dynamics: Law 18/2020 updates, consumer debt burden limits, BNPL frameworks, and retail credit regulations. DROPPED Sovereign Debt & Treasuries: Egyptian government Treasury Bill (T-bill) and Treasury Bond (T-bond) auction results.
COMPETITORS Competitor Intelligence: Product launches, merchant partnerships, expansions, and debt issuances by valU, MNT-Halan, Souhoola, Contact, Aman, Sympl, Blnk, Fawry, Shahry. DROPPED Commodities & Shipping: Food prices, poultry, wheat tenders, fertilizer, Suez Canal freight rates, and oil indices.
INFRASTRUCTURE Credit & Identity: I-Score bureau regulations, e-KYC digital onboarding, OTP verification rules, and digital signature compliance. DROPPED Academic & Ceremonial: University hackathons, "Got Talent" contests, student competitions, and internal company awards.
CAPITAL Securitization & Markets: Securitized bond tranches by consumer finance companies and weekly EGX30 benchmark recap. DROPPED Traditional Insurance: Marine, cargo, fire, and agricultural insurance syndication pools unrelated to retail lending.
FINTECH Payment Infrastructure: Digital wallets, payment aggregators, and national payment rails (InstaPay) affecting consumer transactions. DROPPED Clickbait & Stubs: Rumor pieces lacking dates, concrete financial figures, or official verification.

3 The 9-Stage Pipeline: Ingestion & Preprocessing

The core pipeline is implemented as a compiled state graph using LangGraph. Execution moves deterministically through 9 specialized functional nodes, accumulating verified metadata in a strongly-typed state object (AgentState).

START ──► [1. collect_news] ──► [2. preprocess_news] ──► [3. classify_articles] │ ▼ [4. deduplicate_articles] ──► [5. verify_sources] ──► [6. filter_important_news] │ ┌────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┐ ▼ (has_news) ▼ (no_news) [7. summarize_news] [handle_no_news] │ │ ▼ ├──► (skip mode) ──► END [8. format_email] ◄─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ (send_empty mode) │ ▼ [9. send_email] ──► END
STAGE 1 Resilient Multi-Source News Ingestion
app/graph/nodes/collect.py

Runs all collectors concurrently. Ingestion failures from any single publisher are isolated so the overall workflow never crashes:

  • Tier 1 Official FRA Scraper: Directly scrapes official press releases, circulars, and supervisory manuals from the Financial Regulatory Authority portal (fra.gov.eg).
  • SearchManager Primary Engine: Dispatches targeted searches via SerpAPI for monitored competitors (e.g., valU, Souhoola, MNT-Halan, Blnk) and regulatory keywords.
  • Zero-Cost Fallback Retrieval Engine: If SerpAPI quota is exhausted, rate-limited, or disabled, the SearchManager automatically activates fallback retrieval:
    • Dynamic query-based Google News RSS endpoints (zero API cost).
    • Publisher direct RSS feeds (Amwal Al Ghad, Daily News Egypt, Al Borsa News, Economy Plus).
    • Direct section scrapers (Enterprise Egypt, Techpoint Africa).
Inputs: Collection window timestamps
Outputs: Deduplicated raw articles saved to DB
Failure Defense: Per-source async isolation
STAGE 2 Text Sanitization, Encoding & Full-Text Hydration
app/graph/nodes/preprocess.py

Raw web articles and RSS feeds often contain noisy HTML, broken encodings, or truncated teasers. This node cleans and enriches text before any LLM processing:

  • Boilerplate Stripping: Removes ads, tracking pixels, cookie notices, and navigation chrome using BeautifulSoup and Trafilatura.
  • Unicode NFC & Mojibake Resolution: Corrects Arabic character encoding corruptions (converting UTF-8/Windows-1256 mojibake into clean Unicode NFC).
  • Full-Text Article Hydration: RSS feeds often supply only a 1-sentence teaser. The hydrator identifies truncated articles and fetches the complete body text from the canonical URL.
  • Content Hash Generation: Generates a SHA-256 cryptographic hash of the sanitized body text for downstream deduplication.
Inputs: raw_articles
Outputs: Sanitized clean_articles
Integrity Check: Content SHA-256 generation

Classification Funnel & 5-Tier Deduplication

4 Classification Funnel & Deduplication

Raw financial news volume is substantial. Sending every ingested article to an LLM is both slow and expensive. The agent employs an efficient 3-stage filtration funnel followed by a 5-tier deduplication cascade.

STAGE 3 Multi-Stage Classification Funnel (Rule + LLM)
app/graph/nodes/classify.py

Combines zero-cost deterministic rules with Google Gemini Flash to classify and score candidates:

  • Stage 1 — Deterministic Keyword Pre-Filter (Zero Token Cost): Evaluates articles against compiled regex patterns. Articles containing zero consumer finance/regulatory keywords, or matching out-of-scope triggers (e.g., CBE rates, student contests, grain prices), are rejected instantly at zero token cost.
  • Stage 2 — Deterministic Domain Rules: Flags high-confidence matches (e.g., direct FRA portal items automatically receive 100 relevance and official category assignment).
  • Stage 3 — Google Gemini Flash Classification: Evaluates remaining candidate articles, assigning category (FRA, Consumer Finance, Competitor, FinTech), subcategory, detected entities, competitor matches, a relevance score (0–100), and an importance score (0–100).
Inputs: clean_articles
Outputs: relevant_articles (Score >= 50)
Efficiency: Drops ~70% of noise prior to LLM
STAGE 4 5-Tier Multi-Signal Deduplication Stack
app/graph/nodes/deduplicate.py

In Egypt, a single corporate partnership or bond issuance is often re-published by 10+ financial outlets with slightly altered headlines. The agent collapses these into a single unified NewsEvent using an ordered 5-signal cascade:

Signal Layer Matching Mechanism Threshold / Behavior
1. Canonical URL Normalized URL matching after stripping UTM parameters and tracking tokens. Exact match; instant collapse.
2. Content SHA-256 Cryptographic hash of cleaned body text. Identical syndicated press release bodies.
3. Fuzzy Title Match Levenshtein Token Sort Ratio comparing key terms across headlines. Token Sort Ratio >= 85%.
4. Entity & Time Window Shared named entities (e.g., "valU" + "Amazon") within an active time window. Domain-specific entity overlap.
5. Vector Embeddings Cosine similarity of Google Gemini text embeddings generated on title + first 500 chars. Cosine Similarity >= 0.85.
Cross-Topic Protection Guard: Ensures that distinct official decisions issued on the same day (e.g., FRA I-Score Circular No. 174 vs. Real Estate Valuation Decree No. 191) are recognized as separate events and never improperly merged.
Inputs: relevant_articles
Outputs: Unified NewsEvent clusters
Cluster Authority: Selects most official source as canonical

5 Verification, Idempotency & Summarization

Before news reaches executive leadership, it must be verified for institutional credibility, filtered for high strategic impact, checked against past deliveries, and synthesized with zero AI hallucination.

STAGE 5 Source Verification & Provenance Tiering
app/graph/nodes/verify.py

Leadership must know whether an item is an official decree, verified financial press, or uncorroborated media:

Tier 1: OFFICIAL
FRA portal, Egyptian Gazette, government gazette decrees.
Tier 2: VERIFIED
Enterprise, Al Borsa, Daily News Egypt, Amwal Al Ghad, Economy Plus.
Tier 3: UNVERIFIED
General blogs, aggregators; requires corroboration.
Inputs: events
Outputs: Events with verification badges
Audit: Source authority preserved
STAGE 6 Importance Filtering & Idempotency Ledger
app/graph/nodes/filter.py

Enforces executive selectivity and guarantees that the CEO is never sent the same intelligence twice:

  • Importance Cutoff: Events are evaluated on a 0–100 scale using Gemini Flash. Only events scoring >= 60 (the IMPORTANCE_THRESHOLD) are approved for executive delivery.
  • Database Idempotency Check: Queries the PostgreSQL sent_news ledger table. If an event or canonical story was already sent to leadership in any previous run (whether yesterday or weeks ago), it is filtered out.
  • Conditional Routing:
    • If important, unread events exist ➔ routes forward to summarize_news.
    • If zero events pass the threshold ➔ routes to handle_no_news.
Inputs: events
Outputs: important_events
Ledger: PostgreSQL historical verification
STAGE 7 Factual Summarization & Brand Normalization
app/graph/nodes/summarize.py

  • Stub Rejection: Discards articles that lack substance, concrete dates, or institutional decisions.

STAGE 8 Executive Digest Layout & "No-News" Protocol
app/graph/nodes/format_email.py

Assembles a responsive HTML and plain-text briefing designed for Microsoft Outlook and mobile mail clients:

  • Visual Category Urgency Hierarchy:
    • 🔴 FRA & Regulation: Placed at the top (compliance mandates, licensing).
    • 🟠 Consumer Finance & Competitors: Competitor product offers, merchant deals, securitization.
    • 🟡 FinTech & Financial Market: Digital payment infrastructure, wallets, EGX30 recap.

Currently Active Schedule
🗓️ Weekly Consolidation Via GitHub Actions

Via GitHub Actions (weekly_news.yml), the schedule can be configured or triggered to run weekly by passing lookback_days: '7'. This aggregates all regulatory and competitor events across the full 7-day week into a single executive digest.

Configurable On-Demand / Scheduled
How to Configure Weekly Mode in GitHub Actions:
• Via GitHub UI: Actions → Financial News Digest → Run workflow → Set Lookback Days to 7.
• Via Power Automate Recurrence: Change Recurrence Frequency from Day to Week (Sunday 08:30 AM Cairo) and set JSON body: {"inputs": {"lookback_days": "7"}}.
• Via GitHub CLI: gh workflow run weekly_news.yml -f lookback_days=7.

Production Reliability & Lifecycle Cheat Sheet

8 Production Reliability & Cheat Sheet

🛡️ Rate Limiting & API Pacing

To prevent Google Gemini API 429 quota exhaustion during high-volume periods, all LLM calls use sequential pacing with asyncio.Semaphore(1) and 2–4 second sleep intervals.

🛡️ Pre-Flight Search Circuit Breaker

Before executing expensive search API calls, serpapi_checker.py queries account quotas. If quota is depleted (< 5 searches), it flips immediately to the free RSS/scraper fallback without failing.

🛡️ Isolated Collector Failures

Every web scraper and RSS collector executes inside an isolated asyncio.gather(..., return_exceptions=True) block. If one news portal is down or times out, the other sources continue unaffected.

🛡️ Atomic State Persistence

All database operations use atomic nested transactions (session.begin_nested()). If a single article insertion encounters a duplicate key error, only that row is rolled back.

Pipeline Lifecycle Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet

Step Node Name Primary Action Core Defense / Quality Guarantee
1 collect_news Gathers from FRA, SerpAPI, and RSS fallbacks. Automatic fallback prevents API quota blockage.
2 preprocess_news Sanitizes HTML, fixes mojibake, hydrates full text. Ensures LLM receives clean, complete Unicode text.
3 classify_articles Filters out-of-scope news and scores relevance. Drops CBE/commodity noise at zero token cost.
4 deduplicate_articles Groups syndicated stories into single events. 5-signal cascade eliminates media redundancy.
5 verify_sources Assigns OFFICIAL, VERIFIED, or UNVERIFIED tiers. Enforces clear regulatory vs. media provenance.
6 filter_important_news Applies 60-point cutoff & checks sent_news ledger. Guarantees 100% idempotency (zero repeat emails).
7 summarize_news Generates factual summaries and normalizes brands. Anti-hallucination constraint & brand dictionary.
8 format_email Builds mobile-responsive executive digest. Strips internal scores; categorizes by urgency.
9 send_email Dispatches via Power Automate / Graph / SMTP. Zero IT-admin friction; records run telemetry.
